PayNext Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the PayNext application ("PayNext", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. PayNext helps users create and scan QR-based transfer requests and initiate USDT transfers through compatible third-party wallets on the Polygon network.

Non-Custodial Application

PayNext is a non-custodial application. PayNext does not hold, store, custody, or control users' cryptocurrency or private keys. Transfers are approved and signed by you in your own third-party wallet.

Seed Phrases and Private Keys

PayNext does not request or store wallet seed phrases or private keys. PayNext will never ask you to enter your seed phrase or private key. If anyone asks for this information on behalf of PayNext, do not share it.

Information We May Process

To provide QR-based transfer functionality, PayNext may process information such as:

  • Wallet addresses
  • Transfer request information
  • Transfer amounts
  • References or notes included in a transfer request
  • Blockchain transaction identifiers (transaction hashes)

This information is used to create, display, and complete transfer requests that you choose to make or scan.

Wallet Connections

Wallet connections are facilitated through WalletConnect and compatible third-party wallets. When you connect a wallet, information needed to establish the connection and request transaction approval, such as your wallet address and transaction details, is exchanged between PayNext, WalletConnect, and your wallet.

Public Blockchain Networks

Blockchain transactions occur on public blockchain networks, such as Polygon. Transaction information, including wallet addresses, amounts, and transaction identifiers, may therefore be publicly visible and permanently recorded. PayNext cannot modify or delete information recorded on a blockchain.

Camera Access

Camera access is used to scan QR codes when you choose the QR scanning feature. You can grant or deny camera permission through your device settings. If camera access is denied, the QR scanning feature may not be available.

Third-Party Services

Third-party wallet providers, WalletConnect, blockchain networks, and other third-party services used with PayNext have their own privacy practices. PayNext is not responsible for how these services collect, use, or share information. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by PayNext. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your wallet, device, seed phrase, and private keys secure.

Your Choices

  • You choose which wallet to connect and can disconnect it at any time from the app or your wallet.
  • You approve or reject every transaction in your own wallet.
  • You can allow or deny camera access in your device settings.
  • You can stop using PayNext and uninstall the app at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
